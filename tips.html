Good to know

Tips for buyers and sellers

Straightforward advice from Elliott — and don't forget: we can offer a discount off the standard commission, which can help pay your legal fees.

1

Be a little patient

For buyers or sellers, we are willing to reduce our commission if it will help you — especially if you're willing to do some of the work yourself. Good deals reward a little patience.

2

There are more ways to close than you think

Many people are out there looking to buy and sell, and timing matters. Tools like holding the deed in escrow can help facilitate the outcome everyone wants. We'll walk you through the options.

3

It's simpler than it seems

Keep in mind, there are a lot of ways to do what you'd like to do. It's not quite as complicated as you might think — especially with an agent guiding you through each step.
